DOUBLE MURDER IN CAMPBELLSPORT. UNIMAGINABLE BRUTALITY SHOCKS THE COMMUNITY

November 23 2025, Campbellsport, Wisconsin

Residents of Campbellsport awoke today under the shadow of one of the most shocking crimes in the area's history. As a result of events that unfolded during the night of November 21–22, Sergeant Michael Harper and 27-year-old Elwira Kowalski, who had previously been reported missing, both lost their lives.
The Wisconsin State Police are investigating the case as a double homicide, the circumstances of which officials have described as “exceptionally brutal.”
According to preliminary findings, Sergeant Harper was dispatched to a motel along US-45 following reports of a young woman who appeared disoriented and in need of assistance. It was the last confirmed contact the officer had with dispatch.
Several hours later, his body was discovered near Sandy Road.
A short distance away, investigators also found the body of Elwira Kowalski, who had disappeared several days earlier while returning home from work. Emergency responders at the scene pronounced her dead.

Although authorities have not released details from the autopsies, sources close to the investigation claim that the injuries sustained by both victims were among the most severe local law enforcement has encountered in many years. “There was no doubt that extreme violence had taken place,” said an anonymous officer involved in securing the scene. Residents reported seeing numerous police vehicles, forensic units, and emergency responders in the area, which remained cordoned off for several hours as investigators processed evidence. The investigation is being overseen by Commissioner Gabriel Tassoulis of the Wisconsin State Police.
“At this stage, we are examining every possible scenario. We cannot rule out the involvement of more than one individual. Our priority remains establishing exactly what happened and identifying those responsible for this tragedy,” Tassoulis stated during a press briefing.
Due to the severity of the violence involved, authorities have declined to release most details regarding the victims' injuries or the precise circumstances under which they were found.

Speculation about the perpetrator spread rapidly across social media. Some residents fear the crime may have been committed by an exceptionally dangerous offender operating within Fond du Lac County. Others have pointed to similarities with several unresolved incidents that have occurred along regional highways in recent years.
Authorities are urging the public to remain calm and refrain from spreading unverified information.

Flowers, candles, and handwritten messages have already appeared outside the Campbellsport Police Department in memory of Sergeant Harper. The family of Elwira Kowalski has asked the media and the public to respect their privacy during this difficult time.
